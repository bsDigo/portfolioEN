ETP & DFD document generator

I built a document generator with Python and the Gemini API for two Brazilian public procurement documents: Preliminary Technical Studies (ETP) and Demand Formalization Documents (DFD). It also includes an audit step to support compliance review.

In my experience, preparation time fell from roughly six hours to 30–40 minutes. This is my own estimate; the responsible team still reviews and validates the final documents. I’m developing support for risk maps (MR) and terms of reference (TR).
